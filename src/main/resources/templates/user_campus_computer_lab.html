Computer Science Hub

Where innovation meets education — coding, robotics, AI & beyond.

The Computer Lab is a state‑of‑the‑art facility located in the STEM wing of the Main Building. It features 60 high‑performance workstations with dual monitors, dedicated servers, and a 1 Gbps fiber internet connection. The lab supports programming, data science, 3D modeling, and machine learning projects.

In 2023, we expanded with a Robotics & AI Lab equipped with Arduino kits, Raspberry Pi clusters, drones, and a VR simulation area. The lab also hosts weekly coding bootcamps, hackathons, and guest lectures from industry professionals.
