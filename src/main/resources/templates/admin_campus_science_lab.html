The Science Labs

Inspiring curiosity and discovery through hands-on learning.

The science labs are located in the STEM wing of the Main Building, featuring 6 specialized laboratories for chemistry, biology, physics, and computer science. Each lab is equipped with industry-standard instruments, fume hoods, and dedicated workstations for individual and group experiments.

In 2022, the labs were upgraded with interactive smart boards, wireless data logging sensors, and a 3D printing station. The facility also includes a preparation room, chemical storage, and a greenhouse attached to the biology lab for plant studies.
