About the Main Building

The iconic Main Building has been the heart of our school since 1926. Restored in 2018, it combines neo‑classical charm with state‑of‑the‑art learning spaces, laboratories, and a 500‑seat auditorium.

Surrounded by 12 acres of green campus, the building houses the administrative offices, the main library, and the STEM innovation hub.

  • 34 modern classrooms & 6 science labs
  • Media center with 25,000+ volumes
  • Accessible entrances & elevator
  • Solar panels & green roof initiative
