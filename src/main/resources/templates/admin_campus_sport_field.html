The Sports Field

Where teamwork, discipline, and school spirit come alive.

The sports field complex is a premier athletic facility located just behind the Main Building. It features a full-size soccer/football pitch with natural grass, a 400m 8-lane track, and dedicated areas for field events. The complex also includes outdoor basketball and tennis courts, all surrounded by a scenic campus backdrop.

In 2021, the field underwent a major upgrade with new LED floodlights, a digital scoreboard, and enhanced drainage systems, making it one of the most modern school athletic facilities in the region. The field hosts inter-school tournaments, PE classes, and community sports events.
