The Library

A sanctuary for learning, discovery, and community since 1926.

The library occupies the entire south wing of the Main Building. With over 25,000 volumes, a vast digital repository, and dedicated spaces for quiet study, collaborative work, and archival research, it serves as the intellectual heart of our school.

In 2022, the library underwent a major renovation to add a digital media lab, a recording studio, and an expanded children's literature section, while preserving the historic reading room with its original oak shelves and stained glass.
